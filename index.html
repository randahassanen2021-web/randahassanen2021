Grammar guide

The 12 English tenses, made simple

English has three times (past, present, future) and four forms of each: simple, continuous, perfect and perfect continuous. Start with the table, then open each tense for the rule, examples and a tip for Arabic speakers.

SimpleContinuousPerfectPerfect continuous
PastI workedV2I was workingwas/were + V-ingI had workedhad + V3I had been workinghad been + V-ing
